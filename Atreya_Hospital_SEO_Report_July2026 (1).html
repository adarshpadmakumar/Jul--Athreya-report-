Root Cause Breakdown
3 reasons behind the dip
1. Weekend seasonality — the most likely culprit

Healthcare search behaviour has a strong weekday bias. Looking at the data pattern: July 18 (Sat) = 88 clicks, July 25 (Sat) = 100 clicks, August 1 (Sat) = 95 clicks. These are all weekend dates. The comparison is weekday peak (e.g., July 10 = 173 clicks, July 20 = 186 clicks) vs. weekend lows. When the comparison window ends on a Saturday or Sunday, it creates the illusion of a sustained drop. The trend is cyclical, not structural.

2. Position regression from 6.7 to 7.5 (late July)

Average position slipped from 6.7 during peak week (Jul 6–12) to 7.2–7.8 in the final week of July. Even a half-position drop at this level has a measurable CTR impact. This is consistent with Google's documented mid-summer ranking volatility, where the algorithm reweights freshness and authority signals. Pages without recent content updates — like Cosmetic Dermatology, About Us, and most department pages — are most susceptible to this kind of ranking drift.

3. Monsoon season + Kerala healthcare patterns

Late July into August coincides with the peak of Kerala's south-west monsoon. Historically, in-person hospital footfall dips slightly during heavy monsoon periods, and this often correlates with a parallel softening in local search volume. This is a well-documented cyclical pattern in Kerala healthcare, not an anomaly unique to Atreya. Traffic typically normalises post-monsoon (September–October).

Structural Website Issues
What the website is doing to make things worse
The cyclical factors above are temporary. But the website is carrying structural issues that compound every natural dip — and actively suppress performance that should be much higher given the site's visibility.
Critical technical issues
  • ! Duplicate pages: /ent vs /ent-2, /cardiology vs /cardiology-2, /pulmonology vs /pulmonology-2, /nutritional-therapy and /departments/nutritional-therapy — all sending Google conflicting signals and splitting link equity.
  • ! Ghost URL paths in GA4: Hundreds of broken path combinations like /general-surgery/ent-2/, /vascular-surgery/nutritional-therapy/ being crawled and creating crawl budget waste.
  • ! Elementor bloat: The site runs on Elementor 4.2.0 with a custom WordPress theme. Elementor-built pages consistently underperform on Core Web Vitals — particularly LCP and CLS on mobile.
  • ⚠ Exposed CMS paths: /wp-content/themes/atreya/ appears in production URLs — indicating the theme was not built to production standards.
CTR & content gaps
  • ⚠ Cosmetic Dermatology: 39,088 impressions but only 0.68% CTR (265 clicks). This is the single biggest untapped asset on the site — a meta title/description rewrite alone could triple traffic.
  • ⚠ About Us page: 17,209 impressions at 0.59% CTR. Minimal descriptive copy in search snippet. Should be building trust signals — NABH accreditation, founding story, DMRI credentials.
  • ⚠ Contact Us page: 23,427 impressions at 1.09% CTR. This is a high-intent page (users ready to call or book) with a meta description that doesn't communicate urgency or ease of booking.
  • i Near-zero blog content: Only 1–2 blog articles indexed. No topical depth to support department pages. Competitor hospitals in Kerala are capturing informational queries with content hubs.

Track 1 · This week
Quick wins — high impact, zero dev work
1

Rewrite meta titles & descriptions for 5 high-impression pages

Cosmetic Dermatology (39K impressions, 0.68% CTR), About Us (17K, 0.59%), Contact Us (23K, 1.09%), Neurology (27K, 1.64%), and Departments (26K, 2.87%) are the five highest-opportunity pages. Rewriting meta descriptions to be intent-matched and action-oriented — without changing any on-page content — is the fastest lever to recovering clicks. For Cosmetic Dermatology alone, a CTR improvement from 0.68% to 2.5% means +700 clicks per month.

✓ No development needed ✓ WordPress CMS edit Impact: High Timeline: 2–3 days
2

Add MedicalOrganization + Physician schema markup across doctor pages

Dr. V.T. Haridas, Dr. Ramkumar Menon, and Dr. Vivek Vincent are the three top-performing doctor pages (combined: 778 clicks, 10.5K impressions). Adding structured data (JSON-LD) for Physician schema — including name, specialty, hospital affiliation, and education — signals E-E-A-T to Google and improves eligibility for rich results. This can be added as a plugin or header snippet without touching the theme.

Minimal dev: plugin install Impact: Medium–High Timeline: 1 week
3

Add FAQPage schema to Neurology, Psychiatry, and Cosmetology pages

These three department pages have the highest impressions-to-clicks ratio. Adding 4–5 FAQ items with FAQPage schema (JSON-LD) directly on the page creates eligibility for FAQ rich results — expanding the SERP footprint without gaining a ranking position. This is a well-documented tactic for healthcare pages and has shown 20–30% CTR improvements in similar deployments.

✓ Content + code snippet Impact: Medium Timeline: 1 week
Track 2 · This month
Structural fixes — resolving technical debt
4

Audit and resolve duplicate page pairs with 301 redirects

The site has confirmed duplicates: /ent → /ent-2, /cardiology → /cardiology-2, /pulmonology → /pulmonology-2, /nutritional-therapy → /departments/nutritional-therapy. Each pair dilutes link equity and sends Google mixed canonicalization signals. The fix: pick the canonical URL for each, 301 redirect the duplicate, and update all internal links. This consolidates ranking signals and eliminates crawl budget waste from the ghost paths currently appearing in GA4 (e.g., /vascular-surgery/nutritional-therapy/).

Dev: redirect rules in WordPress Risk if ignored: medium-term ranking erosion Timeline: 1–2 weeks
5

Set up conversion tracking for appointment bookings & calls

The GA4 property currently records form_submit (16 events), but appointment bookings, WhatsApp clicks, and phone call taps are not tracked as key events. This means we cannot measure the true lead-generation value of organic traffic. Setting up GA4 event tracking for click-to-call, click-to-WhatsApp, and form submission with a thank-you page — plus marking form_submit as a Key Event — is essential before any CRO work begins. We're currently flying without instruments.

Dev: GA4 GTM configuration Impact: Foundation for all future decisions Timeline: 1 week
6

Publish 2 SEO blog articles targeting non-brand specialty queries

The site has near-zero content depth. The two highest-value non-brand opportunities based on existing rankings are: "Signs you need to see a neurologist" (already ranking page 2 with 1 article — a proper pillar piece would push this to page 1) and "Best psychiatrist/psychologist in Thrissur" (currently ranking #4–5 with limited content). Both queries have purchase intent and local modifier — the kind of content that directly converts searchers to patients. Target word count: 1,200–1,500 words each, with the relevant doctor's photo, credentials, and a booking CTA embedded.

✓ Content only — no dev Impact: Medium, cumulative Timeline: 2–3 weeks
7

Create an International Patients landing page (GCC + UK focused)

The data shows UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait clicking at 5–7% CTR — far above the India average. These are high-intent NRI users researching care for relatives or for medical tourism. A dedicated page covering NABH accreditation credibility, video consultation availability, insurance tie-ups, and a Malayalam-speaking care navigator CTA would capture this segment properly. Singapore (416 active GA4 users) also warrants attention — likely NRI Keralites with family in Thrissur.

Dev: new page build Impact: High value, untapped Timeline: 3 weeks
Track 3 · Strategic
Suggestion - Website revamp the case for acting now

The current website is actively limiting growth

Everything above is working around structural constraints of a website that was built for 2018, not 2026. Every meta tag we fix, every redirect we add — it's maintenance on an architecture that was never designed to scale. A revamp isn't cosmetic: it's the difference between a leaking boat with a bucket and a properly sealed hull.

What the current site costs you
  • !Elementor on mobile: Average Elementor-built hospital site scores 25–45 on Google PageSpeed Mobile. Poor LCP and CLS directly impact Core Web Vitals — a confirmed ranking signal since 2021.
  • !No appointment booking flow: The "Book Appointment" button links to /contact-us — a general contact page. There is no dedicated booking journey, no slot selection, no confirmation. This is the #1 conversion killer.
  • !Zero content architecture: No blog pillar pages, no FAQ hubs, no topic clusters. Department pages exist in isolation. Google cannot understand topical authority.
  • ⚠Keyword-stuffed title tags: Homepage title: "Best Hospitals in Thrissur | Atreya Hospital Best Hospital in Thrissur, Neuro Surgery Hospital in Thrissur" — this is penalised as over-optimisation in modern search.
What a revamp unlocks
  • ✓Mobile-first build: A properly coded site (Next.js / Headless WordPress) can consistently achieve 80–95 on PageSpeed Mobile — directly improving rankings and reducing bounce on the 88% of traffic that arrives on mobile.
  • ✓Integrated booking flow: A step-by-step appointment form (department → doctor → date → contact) with WhatsApp confirmation can realistically lift form conversions from 0.4% to 2–3%.
  • ✓Scalable content system: Blog, departmental FAQs, patient case studies, and doctor Q&As can be built into the CMS — enabling the content strategy to execute without developer dependency for every new page.
  • ✓Clean URL architecture: Deduplicated department structure, proper sitemaps, schema baked into templates — all the technical debt eliminated at source rather than patched page by page.
