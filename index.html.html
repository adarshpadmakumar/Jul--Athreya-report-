⚠ August confirms the seasonality thesis from the July report

The July report predicted a monsoon-season softening driven by Kerala healthcare search patterns, position volatility, and structural technical debt. All three factors materialised in August. Position drifted from 6.92 to 7.49. Weekly clicks declined from 856 (Aug 1–7) to 637 (Aug 22–28). Then, exactly as predicted, recovery began in the final week — with September 1 posting the strongest single day since July 20.

✓ CTR held steady despite the ranking drop

Even with average position slipping by more than half a rank, click-through rate held at 3.46% (July: 3.49%). This means the traffic that did arrive was still qualified. The dip was not about audience shift — it was about ranking exposure. When rankings recover in September (already underway), clicks will recover with them.

Root Cause Breakdown
What drove the August dip
1. Kerala monsoon seasonality — as predicted

The July report specifically identified late July into August as coinciding with the peak of Kerala's south-west monsoon, and flagged the well-documented pattern of parallel softening in local healthcare search volume. August's decline matched this prediction almost exactly — with the weakest window (Aug 15–28) aligning with the peak monsoon period. Sep 1's 181-click rebound is the first signal of post-monsoon normalisation.

2. Position regression from 6.92 to 7.49

Average position slipped by 0.57 ranks month over month. That may sound small, but at the mid-page-1 range, half a position typically translates to a 15–20% CTR drop for the same keyword set. This is exactly what the click data shows: -16.9% clicks on essentially flat CTR (3.49% → 3.46%). The ranking drift affected priority department pages — Neurology, Cosmetic Dermatology, Orthopaedics — all of which remain undifferentiated in freshness signal vs. competitors publishing regularly.

3. Structural issues from July remain unresolved

The July report identified duplicate pages, ghost URL paths, Elementor mobile bloat, and near-zero blog depth as the compounding factors that amplify every natural dip. None of these have been resolved in the intervening 30 days. Every seasonal dip continues to hit a website that isn't structurally prepared to absorb it. The August decline was more severe than it needed to be — that's the cost of unaddressed technical debt.
